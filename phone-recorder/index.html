  1. High-pass filter (first order, Hz) on each axis removes gravity and slow tilting.
  2. Moving RMS envelope over ms of the filtered magnitude.
  3. Threshold crossing marks a bump; it re-arms when the envelope falls below half the threshold, with a ms refractory period.

This shows the same steps as the VibraSmash pipeline, scaled down to the phone's sample rate. VibraSmash runs them on a 20 kHz piezo signal (80 Hz–2 kHz band, 5 ms RMS window). The phone accelerometer delivers only about 50–100 Hz, so this is a demonstration of the concept, not a measurement of racket impacts. The demo settings were not tuned on data.

Keep the screen on and this page in front while recording. Browsers pause motion events when the page is hidden.
