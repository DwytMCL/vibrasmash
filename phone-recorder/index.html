How it works
  1. High-pass filter (first order, Hz) on each axis removes gravity and slow tilting.
  2. Moving RMS envelope over ms of the filtered magnitude.
  3. Auto threshold (as in the VibraSmash proposal): median of the background envelope plus 6 × its median absolute deviation, never below the minimum. The background is the last 5 s of envelope with bumps left out, so an impact can't raise its own threshold.
  4. Threshold crossing marks a bump. It re-arms when the envelope falls below half the threshold, with a ms refractory period.

These are the same steps as the VibraSmash pipeline, scaled down to the phone's sample rate. VibraSmash runs them on a 20 kHz piezo signal (80 Hz–2 kHz band, 5 ms RMS window). The phone accelerometer delivers only about 50–100 Hz, so this demonstrates the concept. It is not a measurement of racket impacts, and the demo settings were not tuned on data.

Keep the screen on and the app in front while recording, because motion events pause when it is hidden.
